======================
``define()`` constants
======================

These constants provide fixed configuration in ``wp-config.php``. For the
three ordinary controls, a constant overrides any saved WP fail2ban 6 Premium
setting. WP fail2ban Free displays the effective values but does not save
changes; the two debug controls are constants only.
